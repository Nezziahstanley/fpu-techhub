🎓 Student Life

Students Learning Cybersecurity at FPU

A snapshot of how cybersecurity skills are being developed across the Federal Polytechnic Ugep campus.

Network Defense Training

Students learn to monitor, analyze, and defend campus networks using industry-standard tools like Wireshark and Snort in the security lab.

Ethical Hacking Practice

Final-year students practice penetration testing in isolated virtual environments, learning to find vulnerabilities before attackers do.

Cryptography Foundations

Students study modern cryptographic algorithms, from AES to RSA, and implement secure communication protocols for real applications.

Digital Forensics Investigation

Students learn the tools and techniques used to investigate cybercrimes — memory analysis, disk imaging, and chain-of-custody procedures.

Security Operations Center

Team-based training in a simulated SOC environment, where students respond to live incidents, triage alerts, and produce security reports.

Web Application Security

Students practice securing web applications against OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities, learning both attack and defense techniques.

Secure Development Practices

Lessons in secure coding, threat modeling, and DevSecOps pipelines prepare students to build software that is safe from the ground up.

Certification & Career Prep

Students prepare for industry certifications such as CompTIA Security+, CEH, and Network+ with hands-on labs and mock exams.
