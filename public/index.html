About FPU TechHub

FPU TechHub is a portfolio of web-based systems built to solve real problems at Federal Polytechnic Ugep — from AI-powered academic tools to cybersecurity defenses protecting the institution's digital infrastructure.

Developed by students and staff of the Computer Science Department, these systems demonstrate applied learning in modern software engineering, and the accompanying e-book library gives students free access to 200 curated academic texts.
