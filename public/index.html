About FPU TechHub

FPU TechHub is a portfolio of web-based systems built to solve real problems at Federal Polytechnic Ugep — from AI-powered academic tools to cybersecurity defenses protecting the institution's digital infrastructure.

Developed by students and staff of the Computer Science Department, these systems demonstrate applied learning in modern software engineering.
